02 · What drives me

Three fields, one discipline.

Cars, aircraft and computer chips look very different, but all three depend on well-designed electrical systems. These are the areas I'm building toward.

Automotive & EV systems

Low and high voltage systems, battery design and vehicle firmware, from a high school Electrathon car to Georgia Tech's FSAE electric racecar.

Aviation & drones

FAA Part 107 certified pilot. I build and tune FPV drones in Betaflight and design stabilized camera gimbals.

Chip design & digital logic

Digital logic, CMOS networks and gate-level design. I'm interested in how transistors become the processors inside every vehicle and aircraft.
